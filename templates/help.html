flagQuick startexpand_more
  1. Aim the camera. Open Config and move the camera until your whole face is in the picture and the green box follows it. The bar at the top then says Tracking your head with a green flag.
  2. Set the centre. Sit as you drive, look straight at the screen and press Centre (on Status, Adjust or Config). The program also does this by itself after every start.
  3. Check the joystick on the PC. On Windows press Win+R, type joy.cpl and press Enter. "RaspberryPi Head Tracker" is in the list; in Properties the crosshair follows your head.
  4. Bind it in your sim: see In your sim.
  5. Fine-tune on Adjust: see Tuning.
sports_motorsportsIn your simexpand_more

To the PC the tracker is an ordinary USB game controller with two axes:

  • X – head turn, left/right
  • Y – head tilt, up/down

In the sim's controller settings, assign X to the axis for looking left/right and Y to looking up/down. Sims call these differently, for example "look left/right axis", "head yaw/pitch" or "free look".

  • When the sim asks you to move the axis, turn your head to one side.
  • Set the sim's dead zone for these axes to 0 and its curve to linear. The tracker already has its own dead zone and curve (Adjust); both together are hard to tune.
  • View goes the wrong way: use Invert on Adjust or in the sim, not both.
  • Some sims take head tracking only from TrackIR-type software, not from a joystick axis. Then opentrack on the PC can help: its "Joystick input" reads this tracker and passes the movement on to the sim.
dashboardWhat you seeexpand_more
The bar at the top, left: the tracker
  • flag Tracking your head – everything works.
  • flag Setting the centre … – look straight at the screen for a moment.
  • flag No face found – the joystick holds its position for a moment, then goes back to the middle; see Problems.
The bar at the top, right: the settings
  • sports_score Settings saved and in use – nothing to do.
  • Yellow – you changed something that is not saved yet, or that needs a restart. The buttons say what you can do: Save, Back to saved or Try now.
  • Blue – the program restarts or tries new settings. Press Keep within {{trial_seconds}} seconds, or the last working settings come back.
  • Red – a change was undone (the bar says why), or there is no connection.
The pages
  • Status – the joystick position and the numbers behind it, live.
  • Adjust – how head movements become joystick movements: range, dead zone, curve, smoothing. Used right away.
  • Config – camera picture, face model, camera size, joystick device and web server.
tuneTuningexpand_more

All of these are on Adjust and work right away. Watch the dot on the graph while you move your head. Press Save in the bar when you like it.

The view moves too muchMake Range larger.
I have to turn my head too farMake Range smaller.
The view shakes when I hold stillMake Dead zone larger or Smoothing lower. On Config: the NeuralNet face model, or with YuNet a larger face detection size.
Small movements do nothingMake Dead zone smaller.
The view lags behind quick turnsMake Follow speed higher.
The view jerks now and then (latency on Status jumps)With NeuralNet: on Config, set Processor cores to one less than the Pi has (the default). Close the Config page in the browser while driving: its camera picture costs processor time.
Nodding hardly moves the viewUse the NeuralNet face model (Config), or make Range up/down smaller.
Fine near the middle, fast at the edgeCurve above 1.
The middle is off (after moving the seat or the camera)Look straight ahead and press Centre.

Defaults on Adjust puts these values back to where they started.

wifiWi-Fiexpand_more

When the tracker is on no Wi-Fi network – the first time, or when the known network is gone – it opens its own Wi-Fi called HeadTracker-… (open, no password).

  1. Connect your phone or PC to HeadTracker-…. Most phones then show Sign in to network: tap it and the setup page opens. Otherwise open http://10.42.0.1/setup – on a phone, turn off mobile data first: the tracker's Wi-Fi has no internet, so the phone would send the page's requests over mobile data, and the list of networks stays empty.
  2. Pick your network, type its password and press Join.
  3. The tracker's own Wi-Fi closes. Connect your phone or PC to your network again and open the tracker by its name, for example http://{{hostname}}.local:{{port}}. Some Android phones do not know these names: look for the tracker in your router's list of devices.

If the password was wrong, the tracker's own Wi-Fi comes back after about a minute, and the setup page says what went wrong.

To move to another network, use Change Wi-Fi on Config. Forget there makes the tracker open its own Wi-Fi again.

The tracker cannot join company networks that need a user name, or old WEP networks.

buildProblemsexpand_more
No face found
  • Light your face from the front; a bright window behind you makes the face too dark.
  • Your whole face must be in the picture (Config), not too small: sit closer or aim the camera better.
  • Strong reflections on glasses can disturb the tracker; try a slightly different angle.
The PC does not see the joystick
  • Use a data cable, not a charge-only cable, in the Pi's USB-C port.
  • The Pi's USB port must be set up as a device and the joystick created (README, setup steps 1 and 4).
The joystick moves, the view in the sim does not
  • Check the binding in the sim (In your sim); some sims only read controllers that were connected before the sim started.
New camera or connection settings were undone
  • New settings are only tried first. If the camera, the joystick or the web server does not start with them, or you do not press Keep within {{trial_seconds}} seconds, the last working settings come back. The bar says why.
The page is gone after changing the port
  • Open the page at the new port. If that port does not work, the program goes back to the old one after {{trial_seconds}} seconds.
No connection to the tracker
  • The program is not running or the network is down. On the Pi, systemctl status headtracker shows whether it runs, and journalctl -u headtracker -b shows its log.
